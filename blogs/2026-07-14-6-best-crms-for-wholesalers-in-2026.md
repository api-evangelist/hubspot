Wholesale businesses require more than a generic customer relationship management (CRM) system. A CRM for wholesalers must support account-specific pricing, large product catalogs, repeat orders, and sales workflows that integrate with inventory and fulfillment systems. When these processes are disconnected, quoting slows, errors increase, and revenue opportunities are harder to capture.
