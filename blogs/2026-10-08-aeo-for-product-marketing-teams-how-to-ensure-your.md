Product marketing’s core job is positioning: Deciding how a product should be described, what category it competes in, and which differentiators matter most to buyers. The results are deployed through sales, content, and campaigns. But now there’s a channel that interprets and rebroadcasts your positioning at scale without asking permission: answer engines.
