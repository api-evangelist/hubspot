Scrunch and Ahrefs Brand Radar both track brand visibility in AI answers, but they solve different problems. Scrunch is purpose-built for AEO: it monitors AI citations, audits AI crawlability, and actively optimizes how AI bots experience your site. Ahrefs Brand Radar is an add-on to the broader Ahrefs SEO platform, built for teams that want AI visibility alongside traditional search data, backlinks, and web mentions in one place.
